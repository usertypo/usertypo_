1. Who We Are

usertypo_ is a free online typing platform built for people who care about speed, accuracy, and the feel of every keystroke. Whether you are warming up for work, chasing a personal best, or racing a friend in real time, usertypo_ is designed to stay out of your way and keep the focus on typing.

We ship the Service at usertypo.com on Cloudflare Pages. Real-time multiplayer and leaderboards run on Cloudflare Workers. Accounts use Clerk; app data lives in Supabase Postgres. The product is operated by usertypo_.

2. Our Story

usertypo_ started with a simple frustration: we loved typing tests, but most of them felt either too bare or too cluttered. Solo practice was easy to find. Smooth, modern multiplayer—with a UX that felt intentional instead of bolted on—was harder.

So we set out to build the site we wanted to use ourselves: a multiplayer-first typing experience with a clean, contemporary interface. From day one the goal was not just “another WPM counter,” but a place where you could race friends, climb leaderboards, track progress over time with detailed user stats, and still enjoy a quiet solo session when you needed one.

Late nights turned into prototypes. Prototypes turned into rooms, duel matches, profiles, and themes. On July 27, 2026, usertypo_ officially went live—and we have been iterating ever since.

3. Mission & Values

Our mission is to make typing practice feel fast, fair, and genuinely fun—whether you are alone at the keyboard or racing someone across the world, all while providing detailed user stats to track your growth.

  • UX first. Clarity, responsiveness, and comfort matter as much as raw features.
  • Fair play. Leaderboards and races should reward skill, not cheats or noise.
  • Accessible by default. Core typing and racing features stay free so anyone can improve.
  • Open and honest. We ship in the open, document how the product works, and listen to feedback.

4. What We Build

usertypo_ combines classic typing practice with social and competitive layers:

  • Timed and word-count typing tests with languages, punctuation, and numbers
  • Real-time multiplayer rooms and duel matches
  • Profiles, XP, streaks, and detailed session stats
  • Friends, notifications, and online presence
  • Leaderboards with privacy-aware visibility controls
  • Themes, sound packs, and customization that stay out of the way until you want them

Features continue to evolve. If something feels missing, tell us—we read contactus@usertypo.com and in-app contact messages carefully.

5. Design Philosophy

Typing is a rhythm. The UI should protect that rhythm, not interrupt it. We aim for a modern look with deliberate motion, strong focus states, and layouts that keep the caret and text as the hero of the screen.

Multiplayer should feel instant and readable under pressure. Settings should be powerful without dumping every option onto the first screen. When we add something new, we ask whether it helps you type better—or just makes the page louder.

6. How We Stand Out

usertypo_ distinguishes itself by offering an unparalleled combination of precise analytics and an engaging environment. We provide detailed user stats that empower you to understand your typing habits on a granular level. These include:

  • Speed Metrics: Track your WPM (Words Per Minute) in real-time, allowing you to monitor your peak performance and average pacing.
  • Accuracy Tracking: Analyze your error rates and pinpoint specific keys or patterns where mistakes frequently occur.
  • Consistency Analysis: Review how steady your typing rhythm remains throughout a session, highlighting areas for flow improvement.
  • Historical Trends: Visualize your progress over days, weeks, and months to see the direct results of your practice.

Moreover, we pair these comprehensive analytics with a robust multiplayer feature, allowing you to compete against friends or other typists globally. All of this is seamlessly integrated into a clean UI that minimizes distractions and keeps your focus entirely on the keyboard.

7. How Scoring Works

After every test, usertypo_ summarizes your run with four headline numbers. Here is the short version of what each one means—and the formulas behind them.

WPM (net speed)

Correct characters only, divided into five-character “words,” then divided by minutes typed. Mistakes on a finished word can remove that whole word from the net count, so clean typing is rewarded. Elapsed time is floored at two seconds so very short samples do not explode.

WPM = ( validChars ÷ 5 ) elapsedMinutes
where
elapsedMinutes = max( elapsedMs ÷ 60000 , 2 ÷ 60 )

Accuracy

A keystroke ratio—not a “words that look correct” ratio. Fixing typos still costs keystrokes, so accuracy reflects control over the keyboard stream.

accuracy% = max( 0 , totalKeystrokes − errorsMade totalKeystrokes × 100 )

Consistency

How steady your per-second raw speed was. Bursty surges and stalls lower the score; a smooth pace raises it. The mapping is a kogasa-style transform of the coefficient of variation into a 0–100 value.

cov = stdDev( raw WPM / sec ) mean( raw WPM / sec )
consistency = clamp( round( 100 × ( 1 − tanh( cov + cov³÷3 + cov⁵÷5 ) ) ) , 0 , 100 )

Raw WPM (all typed characters, including mistakes), home-page basic/advanced graphs, Profile cards, XP levels, and leaderboard ranks build on these same foundations with more rules and edge cases.
