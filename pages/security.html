Security Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

We take the security and integrity of usertypo_ seriously. If you have found a vulnerability that could affect our users, accounts, races, leaderboards, or infrastructure, please report it as soon as possible so we can investigate and fix it. Questions: contactus@usertypo.com.

1. Our Commitment

usertypo_ is a typing and multiplayer platform. That means we handle account sessions, profile data, race state, and leaderboard scores. We aim to keep those systems trustworthy: users should be able to sign in safely, race fairly, and trust that private account tools stay private.

Responsible researchers help us do that. We welcome good-faith reports that improve the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of the Service.

2. How to Disclose a Vulnerability

For vulnerabilities that impact usertypo_ services, email a private report to contactus@usertypo.com. Please do not open a public GitHub issue for security problems.

For ordinary product bugs, typos, UX issues, or feature ideas that are not security issues, use normal channels instead: GitHub issues on usertypo/usertypo_, or the in-app Contact form.

A shorter version of this policy also lives in the repository as SECURITY.md.

3. What to Include

Include as much detail as possible so we can reproduce and prioritize the issue. At a minimum, please send:

  • A short description of the vulnerability
  • Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
  • Impact — what an attacker could do if the issue were abused
  • Screenshots, request/response examples, or other supporting evidence when useful
  • Your preferred contact method if follow-up is needed

Please do not include real user passwords, session tokens from other people, or unrelated personal data in your report.

4. Submission Guidelines

Test carefully and stay within good-faith research:

  • Do not perform actions that could cause a denial of service, overload multiplayer rooms, spam leaderboards, or put significant strain on critical resources.
  • Do not negatively impact other users. Use your own accounts and test rooms wherever possible.
  • Do not attempt to access, modify, or delete another person’s account, friend list, race history, or private data beyond what is needed to demonstrate the issue.
  • Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have had a reasonable chance to investigate and ship a fix.
  • Social engineering of usertypo_ staff or support channels, physical attacks, and spam are not part of this program.

5. In Scope

We are especially interested in issues affecting:

  • usertypo.com — the main typing app and static frontend
  • Cloudflare Workers used for multiplayer (Durable Objects) and leaderboards
  • Authentication and session handling (via Clerk), including account takeover paths
  • Unauthorized access to profiles, friends, notifications, typing history, or settings
  • Leaderboard or race integrity issues (for example, injecting scores or spoofing opponents)
  • Server-side request issues, broken access control, injection, or privilege escalation in our Workers and APIs
  • Exposure of secret credentials (service-role keys, secret API keys, private tokens)

If you are unsure whether something is in scope, email us anyway — we would rather hear about it early.

6. Out of Scope

The following are generally not treated as vulnerabilities for usertypo_:

  • Publishable Clerk keys (pk_…) and Supabase anon / publishable keys that ship in the browser by design
  • The ability to fork, clone, or read this open-source repository
  • Missing security headers or best-practice suggestions with no demonstrated impact
  • Reports based solely on automated scanner output without a working proof of concept
  • Clickjacking on pages with no sensitive action, or self-XSS that requires the victim to paste code themselves
  • Rate limiting or brute-force concerns already mitigated by our auth provider, unless you can demonstrate a practical bypass with real impact
  • Issues in third-party services (Clerk, Supabase, Cloudflare, and similar) that are outside our control — please report those to the provider when appropriate
  • Empty or placeholder ad slots before a third-party ad provider is enabled

7. How We Protect the Service

A high-level look at how usertypo_ is built with security in mind:

  • Authentication. Sign-in is handled by Clerk. We do not store user passwords in our own database.
  • Data access. App data in Supabase is protected with Row Level Security where applicable. Secret keys and service-role credentials stay in server / dashboard environments, not in the public client bundle.
  • Open source vs secrets. Making the repository public lets people read and fork the code. It does not grant strangers deploy access, database access, or the ability to push to production.
  • Hosting. The frontend is served through Cloudflare Pages. Multiplayer and leaderboards run on Cloudflare Workers with origin and CORS controls.
  • Leaderboards. Leaderboard writes go through a Cloudflare Worker (Postgres) rather than exposing raw admin credentials to the browser.

More detail on data handling is in our Privacy Policy.

8. What Happens After You Report

We will acknowledge security reports when we can, investigate, and work on a fix before any public disclosure. Timing depends on severity and complexity.

We currently do not run a paid bug bounty program. That may change later. Even without a bounty, thoughtful reports are appreciated and help keep usertypo_ safe for everyone who types here.

If your report is valid and you wish to be credited after a fix ships, tell us in the email and we will do our best to acknowledge you where appropriate.
