Multiplayer Typing Rules

Error cascading — Once you type an incorrect letter, every character you type after it is also marked as an error, even if those characters match the expected text. You must backspace to the original mistake and fix it before you can continue typing correctly.

Why? — This prevents players from ignoring errors and racing ahead. Everyone must correct their mistakes, ensuring that WPM scores reflect genuine accuracy and speed, not just raw key-mashing.

How it differs from solo — In the regular typing test on the home page, errors are handled more leniently. In multiplayer, the strict error cascading system is enforced to keep competition fair for all participants.
