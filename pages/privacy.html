1. Introduction

usertypo_ is a free online typing test platform. For privacy purposes, usertypo_ is the data controller of personal data processed through the Service.

This policy applies to information collected through our website, accounts, multiplayer features, leaderboards, and related communications. By using the Service, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information (via Clerk)

When you create or sign in to an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Username / display name
  • Profile picture / avatar URL (if provided)
  • Authentication method (email and password, or Google sign-in)
  • Data provided by Google if you choose “Continue with Google”

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Clerk. We do not see or store your password in plain text.

2.2 Typing and progress data (Supabase)

If you are signed in, we store:

  • Typing session results (WPM, raw WPM, accuracy, consistency, errors, character counts)
  • Test configuration (mode, duration/word count, language, punctuation, numbers)
  • Personal best markers and failed-test metadata
  • Session diagnostics (error patterns and key-level timing summaries; limited history)
  • XP, level, streaks, daily XP, and related XP event history

2.3 Social and profile data

  • Friends list and friend requests
  • Online presence / last-seen timestamp
  • Notifications (for example, friend requests and acceptances)
  • Blocked users
  • Privacy settings such as profile visibility, friend-request preference, and leaderboard visibility
  • Public profile information (username, avatar, and stats you choose to make visible)

2.4 Leaderboard data (publicly visible)

Username, avatar, WPM, accuracy, and consistency appear on public leaderboards when you are opted in. You can opt out using the show on leaderboard setting in your account settings.

2.5 Multiplayer data

  • Room participation and race results
  • Real-time typing progress during races (used to run the race; not stored permanently as keystroke streams)
  • Connection metadata needed to operate multiplayer sessions

2.6 Local device data

We store preferences and settings in your browser (for example, via localStorage), such as theme and typing preferences. This data stays on your device unless you clear site data in your browser.

2.7 Automatically collected technical data

When you use the Service, our hosting, security, authentication, advertising, analytics, and content-delivery partners automatically receive technical data such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version (user agent)
  • Device and connection information
  • Request logs and approximate location derived from IP (by providers)
  • Approximate country code (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2) for signed-in accounts, derived from CDN edge location data, used only for aggregate community charts on the About page
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see Sections 5 and 6)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure page visits, unique users, and related usage metrics when you choose Accept all in the cookie banner. If you choose Essential only, GA4 is not loaded.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the typing test experience
  • Save your settings, scores, progress, and diagnostics
  • Power leaderboards, public profiles, friends, and notifications
  • Enable multiplayer racing and prevent abuse (including anti-cheat WPM limits)
  • Authenticate accounts and keep the Service secure
  • Improve the website and fix issues
  • Measure site usage with analytics (Google Analytics 4) when analytics cookies are accepted
  • Show anonymized, aggregate community stats (for example on the About page)
  • Display third-party advertisements that help keep the Service free
  • Respond to privacy and support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

This section is our Cookie Policy. We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies as follows:

  • Essential: session and authentication cookies required to keep you signed in (via Clerk); your cookie-choice cookie (usertypo_consent); a compact theme boot cookie (usertypo_theme_boot); a shared custom theme cookie (usertypo_custom_themes) so saved custom themes appear on both usertypo.com and learn.usertypo.com; and, when the Service is served through Cloudflare, security/CDN cookies Cloudflare may set (for example __cf_bm or cf_clearance) to protect the site and deliver content. These are required for core operation.
  • Functional: full typing preferences stored in your browser via localStorage (theme details, caret, sound, language, and related settings). This is separate from cookies and stays on your device.
  • Analytics: if you choose Accept all, Google Analytics 4 may set cookies such as _ga to measure visits and active users. Choosing Essential only turns analytics off and GA4 is not loaded.
  • Advertising: when third-party ads are enabled, advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads that help keep the Service free.

On first visit you can choose Accept all or Essential only. You can reopen the banner anytime via , or block cookies in your browser. Blocking essential cookies may break login and other core features.

Note: Cloudflare cookies are set by Cloudflare’s network when the site is hosted or proxied through Cloudflare. We document them here; they are not created by first-party JavaScript in this app.

6. Third-Party Ads

The Service may display third-party advertisements to help keep usertypo_ free. When ads are enabled, advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Advertising cookies may enable ad partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may limit ads through browser controls and preference tools provided by ad networks, which can reduce but may not eliminate all ads.
  • Ad-network publisher statistics measure ad impressions and revenue, which differ from site visit analytics in Google Analytics.

When a specific advertising partner is configured, we will update this policy with that partner’s name and privacy links. Until then, ad placements on the site may show placeholders only.

8. Data Retention

  • Account data: kept while your account remains active.
  • Typing sessions and progress: kept while your account exists.
  • Session diagnostics: we retain the most recent 50 diagnostics per user; older diagnostics are automatically deleted.
  • After account deletion: associated app data in our database is deleted (including cascade deletes of related records). Leaderboard rankings for that account are removed via our Cloudflare Worker. Authentication records are deleted through Clerk. Some residual copies may remain briefly in backups or logs until those systems rotate.

9. Data Security

  • Data in transit is protected with HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are managed by Clerk; we do not store plaintext passwords
  • Row Level Security (RLS) is enabled on our Supabase tables
  • Server-side secrets are not exposed to the browser

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We work to protect your information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.
